Built around how a school business office already works

Your chart of accounts, your departments, your fiscal year. Apollo follows the rules you set up once.

Line-item purchase orders

Staff list each item with a quantity, unit price, and account number. Totals add themselves up, and vendors are remembered for next time.

Routing by department

Each account belongs to a department, and each department has a head. Orders go to the person who owns the budget, without anyone choosing.

Approval limits by amount

Set dollar thresholds so larger purchases go to the business manager or head of school instead of a department head.

Check requests and reimbursements

Staff submit reimbursements with the receipt attached. Approvers see the image or PDF right on the request.

Unsure of the account code?

Requesters can leave the code for their approver to assign, so a missing GL code never holds up a request.

Numbered by fiscal year

Every PO and check request gets the next number for the July–June fiscal year, with no gaps and no duplicates.

A history on every request

Submissions, approvals, denials with reasons, payments, and voids are logged with who did it and when.

Exports your auditor will accept

Download any request as a PDF with its attachments and history included, or export filtered transactions to Excel.

How a request moves

The same four steps for every purchase order and check request.

  1. Submit

    A staff member fills in the vendor, line items, and account numbers, and attaches a quote or receipt.

  2. Route

    Apollo sends it to the department head or the approver for that dollar amount.

  3. Approve

    The approver reviews it from their dashboard, adds a note if needed, and approves or denies it.

  4. Pay

    The business office records the check number. The amount moves from encumbered to spent.

Know what's left before the invoice arrives

Approved orders count against the account the moment they're approved, not weeks later when they're paid. Set annual budgets per account, and the encumbrance report shows pending, encumbered, spent, and remaining for each one.
